Entity fields, relationships, nullability, and stated constraints were checked against the authoritative proposed logical and relational specifications: [`08_DATA_MODEL_AND_DEMO_DATA.md`](official-specification/docs/08_DATA_MODEL_AND_DEMO_DATA.md) and [`DATABASE_SCHEMA.md`](official-specification/docs/DATABASE_SCHEMA.md). ## CAP model scope

The entity definitions in the official model are sufficient for draft local CAP modeling where fields and relationships are listed. They do not establish HANA deployment readiness, tenant isolation, identity authorization, or final lifecycle contracts. The two unresolved collection types above are not inferred into the CAP model. CAP status values that depend on unresolved decisions remain strings. Production persistence remains blocked by D-13 and platform verification.

## Validation status

The validator now checks the manifest and 157 records in schema-covered collections, covering 45 of 47 fixture collection types. It checks duplicate identifiers and declared/mapped cross-file references over all fixture collections. The two unresolved collection types remain listed by the strict validation command, which exits 1 until the official specifications are amended or the decisions are resolved.
