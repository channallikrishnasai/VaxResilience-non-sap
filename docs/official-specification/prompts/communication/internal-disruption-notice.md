# Proposed Communication Prompt — Internal Disruption Notice

**Status:** Template only; no notification provider or automated sending is implemented or selected.

Prepare a concise internal notice for human review using only authorized, source-linked facts. Distinguish confirmed facts, reported claims, estimates, synthetic scenario data, and unknowns. For the demonstration scenario, describe a 30-day delay, 20-day coverage, and 10-day potential gap as synthetic inputs; state that backup supplier stock is unconfirmed pending checks.

Do not assert a patient outcome, blame a supplier/person, promise a recovery date, or claim an action has been approved/executed unless a verified system state supports it. Include the incident reference, operational issue, known exposure, missing verification, current human decision needed, and source freshness when supplied. Exclude unnecessary sensitive/customer information.

Return a draft only; do not send or trigger a notification. A human checks audience and content under the applicable communication policy. Preserve uncertainty and cite evidence references. Do not expose chain-of-thought.
