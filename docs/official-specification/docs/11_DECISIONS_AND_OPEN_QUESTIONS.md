## 1. Decision handling

“Confirmed” means explicitly established in the project documentation; it does not imply implementation. “Proposed” means a documented candidate direction. “Pending” means the accountable owner must record a decision before the dependent capability is implemented or enabled.

For each decision, record the approver, date, rationale, affected requirements/documents, migration impact, and implementation/test evidence. Do not infer approval from a synthetic fixture, an illustrative API, or a recommendation in this register.

## 3. Confirmed constraints (not open choices)

- BharatMed and all organizations/records in the scenario are fictional/synthetic.
- The project uses the SAP technology baseline; selecting a technology does not verify connectivity.
- The product has eight modules and seven defined business roles.
- AI authority is Read & Analyse, Recommend & Prepare, then Execute with Permission only after explicit human authorization.
- All eight protected action categories require Supply Chain Head final approval.
- Customer allocation policy remains pending; “healthcare-first balanced” is not an applied rule.
- Voice and email are in scope; WhatsApp is not in confirmed scope.
- No real patient records, live supplier/customer data, or actual patient outcome claims belong in the demo.
